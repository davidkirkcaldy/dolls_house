Hand Made Furniture

All the furniture displayed on this page was constructed entirely by hand, using a variety of materials such as plywood sheets, Obeche sheets, wooden dowels, and other craft supplies. Each piece was carefully measured, cut, assembled, and finished to create unique miniature furnishings that bring character and realism to the doll's house.
