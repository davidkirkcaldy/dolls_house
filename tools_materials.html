Tools & Materials

Building a detailed doll's house requires a variety of specialized tools and materials. The right tools make it easier to cut, shape, and assemble the miniature components, while quality materials ensure durability and a realistic appearance. Below is a comprehensive list of the essential tools and materials used in the construction of the doll's house, along with the types of glues that are most effective for different parts of the build.

Tools

  • Dremel rotary tool and accessories
  • Dremel MS20 Moto-Saw Scroll Saw
  • Various sized Clamps
  • Craft knife / hobby scalpel
  • Ruler and measuring tape
  • Razor saw
  • Sandpaper
  • Paintbrushes
  • Mini screwdriver set
  • Tweezers
  • Cutting mat

Materials

  • Wood (plywood, Obeche, MDF)
  • Cardboard
  • Paints (acrylic, water-based)
  • Wallpaper samples
  • Fabric scraps
  • Plastic sheets
  • Miniature hardware (hinges, handles)
  • Decorative trims
  • Foam board
  • Wooden dowels

Glues

  • Wood Glue: For general assembly of wooden parts.
  • Super Glue: For quick bonding of small parts and details.
  • Textile Glue: For attaching fabric and soft materials.
  • Clear Glue: For transparent bonding needs.
  • Craft Glue: For general crafting purposes.
